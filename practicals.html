Class Practicals

Class practicals allow you to experience how psychological research is planned, conducted, analysed and evaluated. You will complete one practical in each of the four contexts and use these experiences to prepare for Paper 2.

Health & Well-being

Interview Practical

Explore how people think and feel about health, well-being, stress or coping through first-hand interviews.

Research method: Interview Start Practical → Human Development

Observation Practical

Observe and record real behaviour as it happens, then code and analyse what you see.

Research method: Observation Start Practical → Human Relationships

Survey / Questionnaire Practical

Design a survey or questionnaire and collect self-report data from a sample.

Research method: Survey / Questionnaire Start Practical → Learning & Cognition

Experiment Practical

Design and run a controlled experiment to test how people learn, remember, or decide.
