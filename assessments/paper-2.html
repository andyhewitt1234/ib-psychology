The concepts

The six concepts can appear across Paper 2. In Section A, you may be asked to explain a concept in relation to your class practical. In Section B, you evaluate the unseen study through two or more of the concepts provided.

Bias Causality Change Measurement Perspective Responsibility

Compact exam strategy

  1. Let the command term lead — Describe, Explain, Compare and contrast, and Discuss each demand something different.
  2. Section A: know each practical — aim, procedure, sampling/sample, sample findings and ethical considerations — and understand the research methodology behind it.
  3. Section B: choose the strongest two or more concepts provided for THIS study, then keep linking your analysis back to specific details of the study.
  4. Budget the 90 minutes — all of Section B and Section A must fit.
