IA Overview & Roadmap

Research Proposal — First Assessment 2027

What the IA is

The IA is a research proposal, not a conducted investigation. You do not carry out the study — you design a proposal investigating a population of interest, using one of the four research methods you have studied through the class practicals.

Experiment Interview Observation Survey / Questionnaire
Final report maximum 2,200 words
Total marks 24 marks
Criteria Four assessed criteria A · B · C · D — 6 marks each

Structure of the report
Title page Introduction Research Methodology Data Collection Discussion References Appendix / Appendices

Introduction, Research Methodology, Data Collection and Discussion are the four assessed IA sections. Title page, references and appendices support the report but are not separately assessed.
