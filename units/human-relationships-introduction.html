Welcome to Human Relationships

Why do other people have such a powerful influence on how we think and behave?

Humans are social. We form groups, friendships and relationships, respond to social pressure, experience conflict, communicate with others, and are influenced by the cultures and communities around us.

In this unit, you will explore why people behave differently in groups, how relationships form and change, and what can strengthen or damage our connections with other people.

What will we explore?

👥 Group behaviour — How social identity, conformity, social learning and group membership influence behaviour.
🌎 Culture & social behaviour — How cultural values, norms and acculturation can influence the way people behave with others.
💕 Interpersonal relationships — How our thoughts, expectations and interpretations influence attraction, relationships and conflict.
💬 Communication & conflict — Why misunderstandings happen and how patterns of communication can strengthen or damage relationships.
🧠 Biological influences — How chemical messengers such as oxytocin and serotonin may influence social behaviour and relationships.
🤝 Improving relationships — How psychological research can be used to reduce conflict and improve relationships between individuals and groups.

Our relationships are shaped by an interaction between the individual, other people, and the social world around us.

Throughout this unit, students should keep asking: Why can the same person think and behave very differently depending on who they are with and the situation they are in?

Imagine this…

A student privately disagrees with an answer, but everyone else in their group confidently gives the same different answer.

Why might the student give the group's answer even though they believe it is wrong?
