Welcome to Human Development

How do we become who we are?

From the moment we are born, we are constantly changing — physically, cognitively, socially and emotionally. Development is not just about growing older; it is about how our brains mature, how we learn to think and understand the world, and how our relationships and experiences shape who we become.

In this unit, you will explore how humans develop across the lifespan, examining biological, cognitive and social changes.

What will we explore?

🧠 Brain development — How the brain matures, adapts, and is shaped by early experience.
📚 Piaget's theory — How children construct understanding through stages of cognitive development.
👤 Erikson's psychosocial theory — How social relationships and emotional challenges shape development across the lifespan.
🌱 Nature and nurture — How genetics and environment interact to influence who we become.
👩 Adolescence — How identity, risk-taking and social relationships change during the teenage years.
📈 Atypical development — How psychologists understand and support individuals who develop differently.

Development is not just about growing older — it is about how our biology, cognition and social world interact to shape who we become.

Throughout this unit, students should keep asking: How do nature and nurture work together to shape development at different stages of life?

Imagine this…

Two children grow up in the same family, with the same parents, but one is naturally more shy and the other is more outgoing.

Why might these two children develop different personalities even though they share the same upbringing?
